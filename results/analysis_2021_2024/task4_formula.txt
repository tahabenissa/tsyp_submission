Effect size of a 12 percent bias

For variables with transform 'difference' (temp_c, rh_pct, solar_mj_m2):
  effect_size = (effect_size_percent / 100) * mean(station value over training period) / fitted_spread

For variables with transform 'log_ratio' (wind_ms, precip_mm):
  effect_size = ln(1 + effect_size_percent / 100) / fitted_spread

fitted_spread = global MAD-based spread from reference calibration fitted on the training period.
effect_size_percent is read from config key 'effect_size_percent'.
